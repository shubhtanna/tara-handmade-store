Our story

Made slowly, in Jaipur

Tara means star. It was also my grandmother's name, and her one gold chain, worn every day for fifty years, is why I started making jewellery.

In 2021 I rented a corner of a workshop in Johari Bazaar and started working with Mohan ji, who has been making jewellery for over thirty years. We now work with three more artisans. Every piece is cut, shaped, soldered and polished by hand, then plated in thick 18k gold or rose gold.

We make small batches, so some pieces sell out and come back weeks later. Thank you for being patient with us, and for choosing handmade.

— Ananya, founder
